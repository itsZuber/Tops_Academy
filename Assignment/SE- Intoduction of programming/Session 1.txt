Question 1:
Write a short paragraph in your own words defining what a 'program' is and give one real-life example from any app you use daily (like Instagram, Zomato, or Paytm).

Answer:
A program is a set of instructions given to a computer to perform a specific task. For example, in Paytm, a program helps users send money to other people by entering their mobile number or UPI ID and confirming the payment.

Question 4:
Write down the steps (as an algorithm, using simple numbered instructions) for ordering food on Swiggy — from opening the app to completing payment.

Answer:
1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Enter the delivery address.
4. Search for a restaurant.
5. Select a restaurant.
6. Choose food items from the menu.
7. Add the selected items to the cart.
8. Review the cart and check the total amount.
9. Proceed to checkout.
10. Select the payment method.
11. Complete the payment.
12. Receive the order confirmation.
13. End.
